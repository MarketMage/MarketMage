## MarketMage

**See the market before everyone else does.** MarketMage is a plugin that makes Claude your e-commerce analyst. It costs nothing to install and start with; you pay only for what you ask. Ask what's selling, what it costs to source, who's undercutting you and what your rivals are up to. Claude checks the live marketplaces for your question and answers with the evidence and what to do next.

- **Website:** [marketmage.io](https://marketmage.io)
- **Plugin:** [MarketMage/plugin](https://github.com/MarketMage/plugin)
- **Install in Claude Code:** `/plugin marketplace add MarketMage/plugin`, then `/plugin install marketmage@marketmage`
- **Support:** support@marketmage.io
